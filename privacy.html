This page explains what personal data The Sip Society collects through this website, why, and what you can ask us to do with it. We keep it short because we collect very little.

What we collect

When you send the enquiry form we receive your name, your email address, the event date, the approximate number of guests, the bar format you picked, and your message. If you email, call or message us on WhatsApp, we receive whatever you send that way.

We use this only to reply to your enquiry and, if you book, to plan and deliver your event. The legal basis is taking steps at your request before entering into a contract, and then performing that contract (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR). Without a name and an email address we cannot reply, so those fields are required.

Who else handles it

  • Web3Forms receives the form submission and forwards it to our inbox.
  • Google (Gmail) hosts the inbox where enquiries arrive.
  • Our web host keeps standard server logs (IP address, pages requested, browser type and time) to keep the site running and secure.

Some of these providers may process data outside the European Economic Area. Where they do, they rely on safeguards recognised under the GDPR, such as the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses. We do not sell your data or share it with anyone for marketing.

Cookies and tracking

This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics or advertising trackers. Fonts are served from our own server, so loading a page does not contact Google or any other third party. The Instagram, Facebook and WhatsApp links take you to those services, and their own privacy policies apply once you are there.

How long we keep it

If an enquiry does not lead to a booking, we delete it within 12 months. If you book, we keep the details for as long as we need them for your event, and invoices for as long as Greek tax and accounting law requires.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you the personal data we hold about you, correct it, delete it, limit how we use it, object to how we use it, or send it to you in a portable format. Email us and we will reply within one month.

If you think we have mishandled your data, you can complain to the Hellenic Data Protection Authority at dpa.gr.

Changes

If we change how we handle personal data, we will update this page and the date at the top.
