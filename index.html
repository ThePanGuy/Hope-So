About

Hopeso brings a bar to the event. The work is the service at that bar: what is poured, how the counter is set, and how guests are looked after from the first drink to the last.

It is for weddings and for other gatherings where the bar should feel like part of the room, not a side table in the corner.

The bar

What people come for is the experience at the bar itself.

Weddings
A bar set for the reception, staffed for the length of the celebration.
